## Design Context

### Users

Developers and traders installing Desic OKX Agent into Codex, Claude Code, and other MCP clients. Experience ranges from command-line power users to people following a one-command setup guide. Their immediate job is to reach a working public market-data tool, with private account setup remaining optional.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm, professional. The setup experience should feel technically capable and trustworthy without becoming verbose, playful, or decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction

A restrained terminal onboarding flow with concise steps, stable output, clear progress, and conventional success, warning, and failure states. Avoid marketing language, elaborate ASCII art, unexplained technical errors, and long tutorials.

### Design Principles

- Reach a verified OKX public connection as the first success, not merely a completed file copy.
- Detect working defaults silently and reveal proxy choices only when the network requires them.
- Keep every step optional or retryable without hiding failures.
- Preserve automation through non-interactive flags alongside the guided flow.
- Never display account secrets or proxy credentials in output and errors.
